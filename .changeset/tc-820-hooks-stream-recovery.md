Stop `HooksService.subscribe()` from crashing the Node process when the shared `/hooks/events` stream fails. The detached stream task rethrew every non-abort error, and nothing awaited or handled the promise, so a ticket mint failure, a 401/403/5xx from `/hooks/events`, or a network error became an unhandled rejection and killed the process under Node's default `--unhandled-rejections=throw` (TC-820). The task now never rejects: recoverable failures — network errors, 5xx, and a 401/403 stream-open refusal, which just means the ticket expired or the key rotated — wait out an exponential backoff (250 ms doubling to 30 s with jitter, reset once a stream proves healthy — delivers an event or stays open ~5 s) and the existing refresh chain re-mints and reopens while subscribers remain. Retries stop when the last subscriber unsubscribes or the service is aborted (sign-out or context abort). A ticket mint the node refuses with a 4xx (other than 408/429) is terminal for that subscription set: the iterator throws the `ServiceError` — carrying `meta.status` — to `for await` consumers, and the stream is not retried. The stream also now honors service sign-out and context aborts while connecting, and ticket material is redacted from stream-open error messages. `HooksServiceConfig` gains an additive `streamRetry` option (`delay`/`wait` hooks) so the backoff clock is injectable. A custom `streamRetry.wait` that resolves instantly still yields one event-loop turn, so a fast-failing stream can never starve the process.
