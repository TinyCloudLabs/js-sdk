"@tinycloud/share-envelope": minor

`@tinycloud/share-envelope/links` now has one state machine for the side that asks OpenKey for an approval (the CLI and Courier, which still use their own loops until they adopt it). Nothing that exists changes, and the package root is untouched.

- `reduce(state, event)` is pure: time and clock skew come in as event fields, and every step returns the effects to run (persist, purge keys, call, sleep, verify, commit, finish). `driveApproval` runs them with an injected clock, store and transport. An entry that is dropped always comes with a purge of the private keys it held, and nothing in an effect or outcome carries a key.
- One table (`RULES`, fed by `classifyReply`) says what each OpenKey answer means. A denied request (HTTP 410 `expired_request` with `status: "denied"`, or the code `request_denied`) ends in `denied`, is never reopened and has its entry and keys removed. A plain 410 `expired_request` is `lapsed`. A 400 `invalid_request` on discover is a refusal of the request and never an expiry. A 401 `invalid_proof` on discover stays kept only until the signed expiry plus 30 s, and is exit 12 only when the local clock is more than 45 s off the reply's `Date` header (otherwise the entry is dropped: exit 5, or exit 3 after expiry). A 401 on fetch or ack never drops an approved result: it is kept until its purge time and reports exit 12 (clock more than 45 s off) or exit 7, with `grantSaved: true` when the grant is already saved (a 401 on the ack). A saved grant whose ack never went through ends `completed` with a warning (exit 0) at its purge time. The purge time wins over every event: a reply that lands after it purges the entry and its keys instead of keeping or holding it. Nothing is kept forever: every entry has a purge time, and a run ends by the signed expiry plus 30 s (one final poll), by the caller's deadline, or, once a result exists, by the 30 s collect grace.
- A resumed approval completes at least one full status check (up to the request limit) before it reports pending, so a slow answer is never reported as pending, and a first check that gets no answer is "unreachable" (exit 6, entry and keys kept). A request created in this run is live by construction, so when the deadline passes before OpenKey answers it returns its link as pending (exit 8).
- `APPROVAL_EXIT` gives one exit number per outcome (0, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11 denied, 12 clock). Exit 8 is returned only with the request's expiry and a live confirmation from this run.
- `parseApprovalFile` reads the one `approval-pending.json` (version 2) and the two files it replaces (the CLI's array and Courier's single object), keeps every entry (one that cannot be read, repeats an id, or claims a request life above 24 h is set aside, not deleted; the CLI's signing key with its `kid` is read as is), and `sweepApprovalFile` removes entries and their keys once they are past their purge time. An acknowledged entry keeps neither a key nor the approval URL.

Not in this change: the CLI and Courier still print and exit as before, and `collectApprovalResult` and `refuse` are unchanged.
