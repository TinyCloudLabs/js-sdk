A sign-in request can include the reserved bot-history key (`BOT_HISTORY_KEY_PATH`) on the signer's own default encryption network with decrypt and key.create, as it can include a secret's own key. Sign-in refuses that key with any other action, on another owner's network, on another network or outside the encryption space.

Details: TC-4322
